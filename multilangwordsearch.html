Word Search Builder (Any Language)

This tool builds a mental-translation Word Search: students see an English word, have to think of its equivalent in another language, then find that foreign spelling hidden in the grid. It works for any language or script - Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, Spanish, Italian, German, and more. No accounts, no server - it downloads as one self-contained HTML file.

Pick a Language example below to see the format for that language, or write your own list. Each line is:

cat = gatto - English on the left of = is the prompt shown to students. The foreign word on the right is what's actually hidden in the grid, split into one cell per character - students have to know or work out that spelling before they can find it.

tiger = 老虎 - multi-character foreign words split one character per cell too (老, then 虎). Single-character words (e.g. cat = 猫) work fine and can be found with a single tap.

A line starting with # is a comment/section heading and is ignored - useful for grouping your list (e.g. "# animals", "# colours").

Choose the Difficulty (this controls the grid size and which directions words can be hidden in), the Word List Display mode - English only (the real challenge: "cat"), Foreign word only (removes the translation step: "gatto"), or Both together (easiest, for vocabulary review: "gatto - cat") - and use Regenerate Puzzle to get a new random layout at the same difficulty and word list. Note: only as many words as fit the grid will be placed - if your list is long, trim it down or the status line under the word list will tell you how many were left out.

The Live Preview on the right is the exact activity that gets downloaded. Students find words by clicking and dragging from one end of a word to the other (either direction works, and single-character words can be tapped once). They also get a Hint button - pressing and holding it briefly reveals the location of one un-found word on the grid.

The Show Answer Key button below is for you only - it is never included in the downloaded file. Save or print it before you close this tab, because it can't be recovered afterwards unless your word list, difficulty and seed are all still exactly the same.
