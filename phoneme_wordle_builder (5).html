Phoneme Wordle Builder

This tool builds a self-contained Wordle-style activity your students can play - no accounts, no server, just one HTML file.

Edit the Word List below to add your own words, or trim the pre-populated list down. Each line is one word, in either of these forms:

thin - a plain English word. It's split letter-by-letter.

θ ɪ n = thin - a phoneme sequence (space-separated IPA symbols, click the palette below the box to build one), with the English meaning after the =.

A line starting with # is a comment/section heading and is ignored (the pre-populated list uses these to group words by phoneme count).

Then choose which word from your list is the Target Word for this activity, set the Difficulty (controls how many guesses the student gets), and choose whether English words are ever shown to the student.

Students see a fixed phoneme keyboard laid out beside the guess board (stacking below it on narrow screens) - the same chart every time, in the same order, so it becomes familiar. Any letters a custom word uses that aren't on that chart appear as extra keys underneath it.

The Live Preview on the right is the exact activity that gets downloaded. In the downloaded game, students get a Hint button - pressing and holding it briefly reveals one letter/phoneme they haven't got right yet.
