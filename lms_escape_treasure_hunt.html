Prefer to generate your own? Use this ChatGPT master prompt

Copy the prompt below into ChatGPT (or Claude), fill in your role/subject/difficulty, and it will generate a themed set of MCQ, fill-in-the-blank, and one-word questions ready to drop into the editor.

MASTER PROMPT — ROLE-BASED QUESTIONS IN A PUZZLE CONTEXT

Generate assessment questions based on a specific professional role and technical subject, embedded inside a themed Escape Room and Treasure Hunt experience.

INPUTS

Role: [e.g. Web Programmer]

Subject: [e.g. React]

Difficulty: [Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced]

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

Create questions that are:

Directly relevant to the day-to-day responsibilities of the role

Focused on practical, applied knowledge

Clearly answerable with no ambiguity

Written so they can logically function as puzzles, clues, or locks

QUESTION TYPES (MANDATORY)
1️⃣ Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)

Generate 5 MCQs

Each MCQ must have 4 options (A–D)

Only one correct answer

Provide:

Correct answer

One-sentence explanation

2️⃣ Fill-in-the-Blank

Generate 5 fill-in-the-blank questions

Blank must be 1–3 words

Provide:

Correct answer

Short explanation

3️⃣ One-Word / One-Term Answers

Generate 5 one-word (or single-term) questions

Answers must be:

A single word, acronym, or short identifier

Commonly accepted in the field

NARRATIVE CONTEXT (MANDATORY)

All questions must be written as if they are puzzles or clues encountered within the following experiences.

EXPERIENCE 1 — ESCAPE ROOM
SETTING (FIXED)

You are trapped in a stone room.

Available objects ONLY:

Several bottles / jars

A locked door

Fire torches

A cipher wheel

A block cipher (engraved or written)

Stone walls and stone floor

No windows. No furniture. No hidden rooms.

OBJECTIVE

Escape through the door.

STATE VARIABLES (IMPLICIT)

TimeRemaining

Heat

CipherProgress

StructuralStress

DESIGN RULES

Questions should plausibly act as:

Cipher wheel alignments

Block-cipher keys

Codes revealed via bottles, firelight, or stone markings

Fire implies risk and urgency

Stone walls/floor must logically justify clues (engravings, patterns, wear)

EXPERIENCE 2 — TREASURE HUNT
SETTING (FIXED)

You are inside a stone sea cave.

Available objects ONLY:

Skeleton

Scroll

Parchment

Fire torches

Treasure chest

Barrel

Stone walls and stone floor

Incoming ocean wave (time pressure)

No tools, no weapons, no additional containers.

OBJECTIVE

Open the treasure chest before the wave floods the cave.

STATE VARIABLES (IMPLICIT)

TimeToWave

TorchFuel

ClueAccuracy

RiskExposure

DESIGN RULES

Some questions should:

Come from the scroll or parchment

Be hinted at by the skeleton (at least one misleading)

Require reconciling conflicting clues

Fire torches affect visibility and time

The barrel must be involved without being broken

QUALITY CONSTRAINTS

Avoid "gotcha" questions

Avoid pure memorization unless role-critical

Prefer:

Scenario-based reasoning

Code behavior

Correct decision-making

Use terminology appropriate to the chosen difficulty
