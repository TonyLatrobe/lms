Phoneme Word Search Builder

This tool builds a self-contained Word Search puzzle your students can play - no accounts, no server, just one HTML file.

Edit the Word List below to add your own words, or trim the pre-populated list down to the words you want. Each line is one word, in either of these forms:

thin - a plain English word. It's split letter-by-letter and the word itself is shown as the hint.

θ ɪ n = thin - a phoneme sequence (space-separated IPA symbols, click the palette below the box to build one), with the English meaning after the = shown as the hint.

A line starting with # is a comment/section heading and is ignored (the pre-populated list uses these to group words by phoneme count).

Choose the Difficulty (this controls the grid size and which directions words can be hidden in), whether to show English words to the student, and use Regenerate Puzzle to get a new random layout at the same difficulty and word list. Note: only as many words as fit the grid will be placed - if your list is long, trim it down or the status line under the word list will tell you how many were left out.

The Live Preview on the right is the exact activity that gets downloaded. Students find words by clicking and dragging from one end of a word to the other (either direction works). They also get a Hint button - pressing and holding it briefly reveals the location of one un-found word on the grid.

The Show Answer Key button below is for you only - it is never included in the downloaded file. Save or print it before you close this tab, because it can't be recovered afterwards unless your word list, difficulty and seed are all still exactly the same.
